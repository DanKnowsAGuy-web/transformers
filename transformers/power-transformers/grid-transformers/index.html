Description

Custom-engineered grid transformers designed for electric utility transmission systems, substations, renewable energy projects, and large industrial power applications. Available in ratings up to 1,250 MVA and 765 kV, these transformers provide reliable voltage transformation, high operating efficiency, and long-term performance for critical power infrastructure.

Manufactured to project-specific electrical and mechanical requirements through our global manufacturing network without compromising quality or performance. Typical lead times are as short as three months, depending on transformer configuration and current production schedules.

All transformers undergo comprehensive routine factory testing in accordance with the applicable IEEE, ANSI, and IEC standards specified for the project. Certified factory test reports are provided prior to shipment. Additional type testing, including short-circuit withstand testing, is available through accredited laboratories such as KEMA, CESI, or equivalent facilities. Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), including live remote witness, is available upon request.

Quotation requirements

Submit the transformer rating (MVA), primary and secondary voltages, application, cooling class, and required delivery date.
