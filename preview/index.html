The problem

You need power. Distortion is quietly aging the hardware that delivers it.

StoryBrand starts with the hero’s problem—not our product list. Here’s the tension operators feel when transformers meet modern loads.

External

Dirty load, clean-nameplate assumptions

Data centers, EV infrastructure, drives, and electronics create harmonics and non-linear current. Transformers sized or specified for “classic” load profiles run hotter, noisier, and harder than the nameplate implies.

Internal

“We just replaced this—why are we talking about it again?”

Nobody wants to explain a mid-life capital surprise. The anxiety is real: reliability risk, unplanned outages, and budget pressure from assets that should have lasted a generation.

Philosophical

Critical infrastructure deserves a full life

Mission-critical sites shouldn’t burn half the life of a multi-million-dollar transformer because the power quality problem was treated as someone else’s job.

½ the life

When energy distortion is ignored, operators can see design life collapse—from roughly 20 years toward 10 in stressed environments highlighted in European research and industry discussion around data-center and non-linear loading.

  • Higher losses and thermal stress under harmonic-rich loads
  • Insulation aging faster than the financial model assumed
  • Replacement cycles that show up years too early
  • Procurement that buys “a transformer” instead of “a protected asset”

The plan

Three clear steps. No maze.

StoryBrand plans are simple enough to reduce fear. Here’s how we work with you.

  1. Name the load & the risk

    Share type, capacity, voltages, application (data center, industrial, utility), environment, and any power-quality concerns—harmonics, non-linear load share, or prior failures.

  2. Spec to protect the asset

    We help frame the right transformer class and options—so the unit is engineered for the stress it will see, not only the average kVA on a spreadsheet.

  3. Source, test, deliver

    One MSA. Confirmed availability and lead time (from ~3 months depending on class). Testing, documentation, logistics, and site delivery under one point of contact.
